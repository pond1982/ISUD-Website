ISUD 2 Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026.

About the game

ISUD 2 is a single-player space boss shooter for iPhone and iPad. The game does not require an account.

Data handled by the game

The game stores gameplay progress, local scores, and audio preferences on your device. It does not send these records to a developer-operated server. Deleting the app may remove its local progress and preferences; device backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

Analytics, advertising, and online services

ISUD 2 has no advertising, third-party advertising tracking, developer analytics service, online leaderboard, cloud-save service, or in-app purchases. It does not ask you to enter your name, email address, contacts, photos, or payment details in the game.

Diagnostics

The game engine may create crash information locally on your device. The game has no developer-operated crash upload service. Apple may handle app diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple's privacy practices. If you send a diagnostic report to support yourself, that report is handled as a support request.

External links and support requests

Opening a support or privacy link takes you to a website outside the game. The website host may process ordinary web requests, such as IP addresses and browser details, under its own privacy practices. If you contact support, the publisher receives the information you choose to send, including your email address, issue description, device details, and any attachments. That information is used to respond to your request.

Children

The game does not include account registration or in-game forms requesting personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change. For privacy questions, contact support@isudzero.com.
