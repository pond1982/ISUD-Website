ISUD 2 · Arcade boss battles

Choose your battle.

Pilot an interceptor into arcade boss battles. Choose from eight 3D bosses and face them in any order. Each encounter unfolds across three phases, with its own patterns and special weapons to watch for.

Hold to fire, drag to move, and tap Pulse when it is charged and you need room to maneuver. Read the warning cues, dodge incoming attacks, and look for your opening.

Reactive fluid effects respond to the action, while new music and sound effects accompany the fight.
