ISUD 2 Support

Help for the single-player iPhone and iPad space boss shooter.

Contact

Email: support@isudzero.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, game version, and a short description of the issue. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, or unrelated personal information. Screenshots are optional.

Choose a battle

Tap PLAY on the title screen, then select one of the eight boss cards. Each boss has three phases. Intro flights and phase transitions return to a steady combat camera before attacks and damage resume.

Move and fire

Hold and drag in the playfield to move your ship and fire. Lift your finger to stop firing. Read attack warnings and move through openings in the patterns.

Pulse

The PULSE button becomes available as its charge builds. Activate it to clear dangerous projectiles.

Pause and audio

Use the pause button at the top right during combat. AUDIO opens independent music and sound-effect controls and pauses the battle. Close the panel and tap RESUME. After the protected countdown, make a fresh touch to move and fire again. Audio preferences save automatically on your device.

Retry and progress

After defeat, choose RETRY or BACK TO SELECT. Scores and progress are stored locally. Reinstalling the app may remove local-only data.

Troubleshooting

If the game is interrupted, return to it and resume from the pause screen. For persistent problems, restart the app and device, then contact support with the steps that caused the issue.
